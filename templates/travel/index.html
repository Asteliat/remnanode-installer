FAQ

Questions we get asked most

Still unsure about something? Write to us — a real guide answers, usually within two working days.

Six travellers and one guide. On Lapland routes in midwinter we take four, because the cabins are small and the light is short. We never add a solo supplement for a group you join — that is our problem, not yours.

We rewrite the day. Every route has two backups — a shorter walk and an indoor plan — and the guide decides which one by 07:00. Nobody has ever been sent home with nothing to do; the record so far is a sauna, a museum and a baker's course.

Most days are 6–18 km on paths, with 200–500 m of climb. If you can walk for four hours with stops, you are ready. Tell us about knees, hearts and fears of heights when you book — we plan around them, quietly.

A 25% deposit holds your place, the balance is due 60 days before departure. Date changes are free up to 45 days before, and moving a trip once costs nothing. Insurance is required, not sold by us.
