Shared inbox, views and assignment

Every workspace starts with one inbox. Split it by market, language, brand or plan — routing rules decide where a conversation lands before an agent sees it.

  • Views are saved filters: “mine, unassigned, breaching in 30 min”. Share them with the team or keep them private.
  • Collision detection locks a ticket while an agent types, so two people never answer the same customer.
  • Merge folds duplicates into one thread; both customers still receive their own reply.

Keyboard: J/K move between tickets, E resolves, A assigns, Cmd K opens quick jump.

Updated 21 October 2026

Rules: conditions, actions and rollout

Rules run on ticket creation and on any update. A rule stops at the first matching action set, which keeps behaviour predictable — chain rules with explicit tags instead of overlapping conditions.

billing_refund.json
{
  "name": "Billing refund triage",
  "when": { "tag": "billing", "idle_minutes": 20 },
  "if":   { "plan": ["growth", "scale"], "language": ["en", "nl"] },
  "then": ["apply_macro:billing-refund", "add_tag:refund", "assign:finance"]
}
  • Dry run shows the last 30 days of tickets a rule would have touched, without executing.
  • Canary runs the rule on 10% of matching tickets for 24 hours before full rollout.

Updated 30 September 2026 · Growth and Scale only

Metric definitions you can defend

Reports use the definitions below by default. Change any of them in Settings → Metrics; historical charts recalculate within 30 seconds and keep a changelog entry.

  • First response — first public agent reply, measured in calendar minutes inside business hours.
  • Resolution — ticket moved to a closed state; reopening resets the clock but keeps the original ticket id.
  • CSAT — one question, 1–5, sent 4 hours after resolution; excluded when the ticket is reopened within 7 days.
  • Backlog age — p50 and p90 of open tickets by queue, refreshed every 5 minutes.

Scheduled exports deliver a PDF or CSV at 07:00 in the workspace timezone. Read-only SQL access runs against a replica, so heavy queries never touch the inbox.

Updated 2 November 2026

API, webhooks and signatures

Base path /v1. Authenticate with a workspace token, scoped to tickets, contacts or metrics. Requests without a scope return 403 insufficient_scope.

webhook.json
{
  "event": "ticket.created",
  "id": "tk_2f91c4",
  "workspace": "northwind-prod",
  "target": "/v1/hooks/lattice",
  "attempt": 1,
  "sent_at": "2026-11-15T09:41:12Z"
}

Each delivery carries an X-Lattice-Signature header: HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body and timestamp. Reject anything older than 5 minutes. Failed deliveries retry at 1 m, 5 m, 30 m, 2 h and 6 h; raw payloads stay replayable for 30 days.

Rate limits: 600 requests a minute on Growth, 4,000 on Scale. 429 responses include Retry-After in seconds.

Updated 11 November 2026

SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning

Scale workspaces support Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace and any SAML 2.0 IdP. Configuration takes one metadata exchange and one test login as an admin.

  • 1. Publish metadata — download the SP metadata from Settings → Security → SSO and import it into your IdP.
  • 2. Map attributes — email, displayName, groups. Group values map to roles (lattice-admins, lattice-agents).
  • 3. Enforce — once enforced, password login is disabled for every member of the workspace, including owners.

SCIM 2.0 endpoint: /scim/v2/Users and /scim/v2/Groups. Deactivation revokes sessions in under 60 seconds; deletion keeps ticket attribution but removes the login.

Privacy policy — summary

Lattice B.V. is the processor for customer conversation data and the controller for account data (name, email, billing). We store EU workspace data in Amsterdam, never sell personal data, and keep support conversations for as long as the workspace exists, then 30 days in encrypted backups.

Legal basis for account data is contract performance; for product analytics, legitimate interest (you can opt out in Settings → Privacy). Data subject requests are answered within 30 days at privacy@latticehq.com.

What we collect

  • Account: name, work email, company, role, hashed password or SSO subject.
  • Usage: page views, feature clicks, aggregate response metrics — no session recording by default.
  • Billing: company address, VAT number, last four digits of the card via our PSP.

Terms of service — summary

Subscriptions renew monthly or annually until cancelled from the billing screen. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stay active to the end of the paid period; annual plans can be cancelled for renewal, not for a partial refund, except where the SLA credit applies.

Acceptable use: no unsolicited bulk email, no reselling the platform as a helpdesk without a partner agreement, no storing special-category data beyond what support requires. Uptime commitment is 99.98% monthly, measured at our edge, with credits from 5% to 25% of the monthly fee.

Liability

Aggregate liability is capped at 12 months of fees paid, or €25,000 for the free trial, whichever is lower. Nothing limits liability for gross negligence, wilful misconduct or data protection breaches caused by us.

Data processing agreement

Our DPA incorporates the 2021 EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK addendum. Sub-processors are listed below with purpose, region and a 30-day change notice. Audits are available once a year, remotely, with 30 days notice, or via the SOC 2 Type II report.

  • Breach notification: without undue delay and within 72 hours of confirmation.
  • Assistance with DPIAs and prior consultations included at no extra cost.
  • Deletion certificate issued within 30 days of termination on request.

Request a signed copy via contact — we pre-sign, so procurement turnaround is typically same-day.
