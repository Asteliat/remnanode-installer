The workshop

A small kiln at the edge of Kalamaja

Kiln & Co began in 2016 with one borrowed wheel and a Sunday market table on Telliskivi. Mari threw forty mugs that first winter; thirty-one of them were good enough to sell, and the nine that weren’t are still in the studio holding brushes and tea.

Today there are three of us, one electric kiln and a glaze table that has been rebuilt twice. We fire once a week, pack with paper and cardboard, and keep the shapes simple enough that you can use them every morning for the next twenty years.

Mari Lehto Founder & maker

Why we fire at 1,240 °C

The kiln has one job: take clay past the point where it stops being clay. Everything else — the glaze, the colour, the sound of a mug on a table — happens because of those fourteen hours.

The glaze table, five years in

Eighty-seven test tiles live in a shoebox under the wheel. Five of them became the glazes we use now; the rest are a record of everything we decided not to sell.
