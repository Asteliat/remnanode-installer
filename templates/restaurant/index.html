The room

One room, twelve chairs, no music you have to shout over.

The room is the old botttega of a carpenter: brick, chestnut beams, a marble bar that seats two and a pass where every plate is finished. Five tables, twelve chairs, one candle each.

We take one sitting a night so the kitchen never rushes and the room never turns. Come at seven, stay until the last espresso.

The people

Marta, Luca, and a very small room.

Twelve seats, one service, three people on the floor. There is no second kitchen and no plan to open one.

We opened in March 2019 with five tables, a second-hand range and a wine list written by hand. Marta cooks, Luca runs the pass, Chiara chooses what we drink. Nothing here is new — the pici are rolled every afternoon, the pecorino comes from Pienza, the Chianina hangs for forty days — but we serve it with a lighter hand than the trattoria of our childhood. Olive oil instead of butter. Lemon where our grandmother reached for vinegar. The room holds twelve.

The menu follows the market at Santo Spirito and the season in Mugello: porcini and new oil in autumn, artichokes and wild asparagus in spring. We buy from six producers, most within thirty kilometres, and we write the menu after the delivery, not before. Everything is cooked to order in one service, so come at seven and stay as long as you like. If something on the list is not for you, say so — the kitchen will find a plate.

Marta Bellandi Chef and owner
